Video contact sheet & thumbnail generator

Mosaic turns a folder of videos into contact sheets, screenshots and animated previews, in batches, on your own machine.

Contact sheets, screenshots and animated previews

Pick any mix of them and Mosaic makes each one for every video in the queue.

  • Contact sheet PNG · JPEG

    A grid of timestamped frames with the file's details on top.

  • Screenshots PNG · JPEG

    Full-size frames taken at even intervals through the video.

  • Animated preview WebP · WebM · GIF

    Short clips joined into one looping preview.

  • Animated contact sheet WebP

    A contact sheet where every frame is a moving clip.

Batch thumbnails for big folders

  • Batch queue

    Drop whole folders, watch each file's progress, and cancel at any point.

  • HDR looks right

    HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision come out with natural colors, not washed out.

  • Stays on your computer

    Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked. Your videos never leave your machine.

Command-line thumbnail generator

mosaic-cli runs the same four outputs from scripts and servers.

CLI reference 
# macOS and Linux
curl -LsSf https://mosaicvideo.github.io/install.sh | sh

# Windows (PowerShell)
irm https://mosaicvideo.github.io/install.ps1 | iex
